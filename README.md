# Global Semiconductor Trade Intelligence

An end-to-end data analytics project that analyzes global semiconductor trade from **2015 to 2025** using **SQL**, **Microsoft Excel**, and **Power BI**.

This project explores international trade flows, supplier concentration, company profiles, export controls, and geopolitical risk. The same dataset is analyzed using multiple analytical tools to demonstrate a complete workflow, from querying raw data to building executive dashboards.

---

# Project Objective

The semiconductor industry plays a critical role in global manufacturing, artificial intelligence, consumer electronics, and national security. This project analyzes semiconductor trade data to identify trade patterns, supplier concentration, company performance, and geopolitical risks.

The objective is to demonstrate how the same dataset can be transformed into actionable business insights using SQL for data analysis, Microsoft Excel for reporting, and Power BI for interactive business intelligence dashboards.

# Business Questions

## Trade Analysis

- Which countries export the most semiconductors?
- Which countries import the most semiconductors?
- Which semiconductor products account for the highest trade value?
- Which trade routes carry the highest trade value?
- Which countries trade with the largest number of partner countries?

## Company Analysis

- Which countries have the highest number of semiconductor companies?
- Which companies are headquartered in the leading exporting countries?
- Which companies generate the highest revenue within their respective countries?
- Which companies operate under high geopolitical exposure?

## Supply Chain & Risk

- Which suppliers dominate the semiconductor ecosystem?
- Which regions depend most on export-controlled products?
- Which hardware categories are most exposed to geopolitical risk?
- Which countries combine strong market influence with high geopolitical exposure?

# Key Insights

- The dataset records approximately **3.9 million units of trade value** (`trade_value_usd_millions`) during the analysis period.
- A relatively small group of countries accounts for the majority of semiconductor trade represented in the dataset.
- The Netherlands is the leading exporting country based on total trade value.
- China is the largest importing country in the dataset.
- ASML contributes the largest supplier trade share, indicating significant supplier concentration.
- Approximately **44.5%** of total trade is classified as high geopolitical risk.
- Export-controlled semiconductor products represent a significant portion of global trade.
- The Trans-Pacific/Suez corridor carries the highest trade value among all trade routes.
